About Abdullah Mandhi & Briyani

Founded in 2015, Abdullah Mandhi & Briyani brings the authentic taste of traditional Mandhi and Briyani to your table. Our recipes are sourced from the finest Yemeni and Saudi chefs, using hand-selected spices aged to perfection.

Each dish is prepared with care, from the slow-cooked mandhi meat marinated in traditional spice blends to the fragrant basmati rice steamed with saffron, cardamom, and cloves. We believe in preserving the authentic cooking methods while delivering exceptional quality to our valued customers.

  • ✓ Authentic Yemeni recipes
  • ✓ Fresh, locally-sourced ingredients
  • ✓ Traditional clay pot cooking
  • ✓ Halal certified
  • ✓ Family-friendly atmosphere
